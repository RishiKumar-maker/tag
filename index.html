How to Play

Driving

  • WASD / Arrows — drive
  • Space / Shift — drift (hold it while turning)
  • E — use your held ability
  • B — switch the ball camera on or off (Car Soccer)

On touch devices the buttons appear on screen. Tap Edit controls on the menu to drag them wherever your thumbs like, resize them, or swap sides.

Tag

Classic: one chaser, tag passes to whoever they touch. Infection: tagged players join the chase — last runner standing wins. The chaser is faster than everyone else.

Car Soccer

Two teams, one big ball. Drive it into the other team's goal: your team's goal is the one in your colour. Three minutes on the clock, and if it's level you play sudden-death overtime. After every goal everyone resets for a 3-2-1 kickoff. Drive over the glowing mint pads for a quick speed burst. There are no power-ups or tagging in this mode.

Power-ups

Drive over a floating item box to grab a random ability — one at a time, press E or tap USE to activate it.
